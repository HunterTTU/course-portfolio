My Top 5 Foods to Cook

  1. Steak

    Steak is one of my favorite foods to cook because I enjoy getting the seasoning and temperature just right.

  2. Tacos

    Tacos are fun to make because there are so many different meats, toppings, and flavors to choose from.

  3. Burgers

    Burgers are simple to cook, but I like experimenting with different cheeses, toppings, and sauces.

  4. Pasta

    I enjoy cooking pasta because there are countless combinations of noodles, sauces, meats, and other ingredients.

  5. Chicken Wings

    Chicken wings are one of my favorites because I can experiment with different seasonings, sauces, and cooking methods.
